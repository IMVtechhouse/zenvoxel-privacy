Privacy Policy

ZenVoxel: Block Puzzle • Package: com.imvtechhouse.zenvoxel

Last Updated: November 2, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

At IMVTechHouse, protecting user privacy is our core foundation. ZenVoxel: Block Puzzle is engineered as an offline-first puzzle game. We do not sell, rent, or collect personal identifying information (PII) from our players.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All core gameplay data is stored strictly on your local device via encrypted SQLite database and private application preferences:

  • High scores, combo streaks, and lines cleared statistics
  • Daily puzzle challenge completions and calendar streak counters ("Daily Stick")
  • Unlocked themes, block skins, and particle effects
  • Audio, natural voice praise, and haptic feedback toggles
  • In-game soft coins and gems earned purely through gameplay

Note: This data remains 100% on your device and is never transmitted to or stored on external servers.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

ZenVoxel incorporates Google AdMob via the Universal Mobile Ads framework to display optional rewarded ads (such as Second-Chance Revives) and non-intrusive interstitial ads.

Google AdMob may process certain device identifiers and performance telemetry in accordance with standard mobile advertising practices:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID/AAID) for ad serving and fraud protection.
  • Analytics & Diagnostics: Ad load status, impression counts, click events, and crash logs.

All data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols. For details on how Google processes ad data, please review Google's Advertising and Privacy Terms.

4. Information We Do NOT Collect

ZenVoxel does not collect or access:

  • Names, physical addresses, phone numbers, or email addresses
  • Precise GPS location data
  • Device hardware sensors, cameras, microphones, or photo libraries
  • Credit card or payment financial credentials (there are no real-money in-app purchases)
  • Social media accounts or contact lists

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

ZenVoxel contains zero graphic content, violence, profanity, or user-to-user chat. It is certified family-friendly for players of all ages. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/EEA), in compliance with COPPA and Google Play's Families Policy.

6. User Data Control & Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

  • Erase Game Data: Clear the game storage anytime via your Android system settings (Settings → Apps → ZenVoxel → Storage → Clear Data) or by uninstalling the game.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: Reset or delete your Google Advertising ID anytime via your device's Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
